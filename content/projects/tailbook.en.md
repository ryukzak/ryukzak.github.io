**TailBook** -- a service that helps people to improve the lives of homeless animals in Montenegro (and in other countries in the future). Like a social network, the app forms a catalog and records the condition of the animal, its feeding, vetting, homing, and provides comprehensive information.

TailBook is an example of solving a social problem in an innovative way and, as an application, is completely user-centered.

We **expect our service to be a successful case of solving a serious problem** through the efforts and cooperation of civil society.
